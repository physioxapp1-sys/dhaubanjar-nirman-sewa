About Us

A construction partner you can hold to a date

Dhaubanjar Nirman Sewa is a full-service construction firm working across the Kathmandu Valley and beyond. We take projects from soil test and structural design through to the last coat of paint — residential, commercial and renovation — with one team accountable for the whole thing.

Every project runs on a fixed scope, a written schedule and a weekly progress report. No hidden variations, no silent delays.

  • Licensed engineers and NBC-compliant structural design
  • Transparent BOQ and stage-wise billing

Our Services

What we do

Four practices, one standard of finish. Each is run by a dedicated project manager with a costed schedule you approve before we break ground.

Building Construction

Ground-up residential and mixed-use buildings — excavation, RCC frame, masonry, services and finishing under a single contract.

  • Structural design & municipal approvals
  • RCC framed and load-bearing systems
  • Electrical, plumbing and drainage

Renovation & Interiors

Retrofits, extensions and full interior fit-outs for homes that need a second life rather than a second plot.

  • Structural retrofitting & strengthening
  • Kitchen, bath and joinery fit-out
  • Modular furniture and false ceilings

Commercial Projects

Offices, showrooms, hotels and warehouses built to open on the date you have already announced to your customers.

  • Fast-track programmes & phased handover
  • Fire safety and compliance fit-out
  • Facade, glazing and signage works

Design & Consultancy

Architectural drawings, 3D visualisation, cost estimation and approval support — with or without the build contract.

  • Concept to working drawings
  • 3D exterior & interior visualisation
  • Detailed BOQ and cost estimation

How We Work

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit the site, listen to the brief and talk honestly about budget before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Design & Estimate

    Drawings, 3D views and a line-by-line BOQ. You approve the number before we mobilise.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Sequenced trades, weekly photo reports and stage-wise billing tied to real progress.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Joint snagging, clean site, documents in a folder — plus 12 months of defect support.
